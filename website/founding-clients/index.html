The founding promise

  1. One person, start to finish.

    The person you brief designs, writes and builds your work. There are no account managers and no hand-offs.

  2. An honest diagnosis first.

    We tell you where the gap is before we sell you anything. It is from £400, credited against any package, with no obligation to continue.

  3. Scope and price in writing.

    You know what you are getting, when it arrives and what it costs before any work starts.

  4. Your project becomes a case study, with your approval.

    We write it with you and publish nothing you have not approved, in words or in images.

  5. If we are not the right studio, we say so.

    Sometimes the right answer is a different studio, or a smaller fix you can make yourself.

Who this is for.

Independent venues in London whose rooms are stronger than their websites.

Owners who want a clear plan rather than a long menu of services. Venues ready to be written up as a case study, and to tell us honestly what is not working.

Who it is not for. Anyone looking for the cheapest possible logo, or a large chain that needs a big team.

Questions we get asked

Why are you offering founding places?

We are a new studio with two projects behind us, and we would rather say that plainly. Founding venues get close attention and a clear plan. In return, we ask to write up the project as a case study, with your approval of every word and image.

What is a venue diagnosis?

A written, honest audit of how your venue appears online, in print and in the room, with a clear order of what to fix first. It is from £400, and the full amount is credited against any package if you go on to work with us.

Do we have to hire you after the diagnosis?

No. The diagnosis is yours to keep and to use with anyone. If you do want us to carry on, the diagnosis fee is credited against the package.

How do you price projects?

Every project is quoted in writing after a venue diagnosis. The diagnosis is from £400, and the full amount is credited against any package if you go on to work with us.
