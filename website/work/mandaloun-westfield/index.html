Mandaloun Westfield

The project

  • Brand refocus
  • Website design

Brief

Mandaloun Westfield is a restaurant with a longstanding reputation for its food, but in recent years its brand identity lagged behind other brands. We were tasked with bringing it back to life.

What we did

This project needed less of a rebrand and more of a refocus. We understood its past success and gave it a younger face, redesigning the website to give both loyal and new customers a new reason to dine.

Result

This project is still underway, but early success is visible in a wider reach and greater awareness of the brand.

This project is ongoing. We will add measured results here as they come in.
