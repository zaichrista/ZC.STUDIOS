The Reach Riverside

The project

  • Brand identity
  • Website design

Brief

The venue was not reaching its target footfall and needed a refreshed brand identity to reach a new audience.

What we did

We restructured the website around the atmosphere of the restaurant and the river, taking a specific cultural lens to ground The Reach Riverside in its mission and target audience. We also remade the logo and applied a new colour palette, both of which are visible across the website.

Result

We are currently building The Reach Riverside into a statement restaurant by Hammersmith Bridge, and the new website is attracting a wider, younger audience.

This project is ongoing. We will add measured results here as they come in.
