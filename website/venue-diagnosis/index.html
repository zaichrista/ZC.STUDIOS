Questions we get asked

What is a venue diagnosis?

A written, honest audit of how your venue appears online, in print and in the room, with a clear order of what to fix first. It is from £400, and the full amount is credited against any package if you go on to work with us.

Do we have to hire you after the diagnosis?

No. The diagnosis is yours to keep and to use with anyone. If you do want us to carry on, the diagnosis fee is credited against the package.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We agree a timeline in writing before any work starts, so you know what arrives and when.

Do you only work with venues in London?

We are based in London and work with London venues first, with most of our experience in West London. If you are elsewhere, ask, and we will tell you honestly whether we can do a good job from a distance.
